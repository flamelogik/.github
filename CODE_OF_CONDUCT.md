# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Logik brings together Flame artists from facilities of every size, all over the world. We want taking part in this community to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that build an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to scripting. Everyone started somewhere.
- Respect different workflows, facilities and opinions. There's more than one right way to finish a spot.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the community, not only for ourselves or our own shop.
- Credit other people's work, including code, shaders and ideas you've adapted.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or demeaning comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information (such as a physical or email address) without their explicit permission
- Sharing client material, confidential project details or anyone's proprietary work without permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the Logik GitHub organization: repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone officially represents the community elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the owners at **conduct@logik.tv**. You can also message an owner directly. Every report is acknowledged within 7 days, reviewed fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

That mailbox is read by the active org owners and by the Logik community leads, who are not all GitHub owners. Anyone involved in a report steps aside from handling it, so a report about an owner is handled by the others.

## Enforcement

Owners are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with it. They'll explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Owners follow this ladder, and can skip steps depending on severity:

1. **Correction.** A private, written note explaining what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
